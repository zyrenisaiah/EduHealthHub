EduHealthHub Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

EduHealthHub is a web-based student health record system for school clinic staff (doctors, nurses, dentists and teachers). This page explains what the site stores and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Staff accounts: name, username, email, an encrypted password, an optional profile picture, and, if you choose to link it, your Google account email address.
  • Student health records: entered by authorized school staff for the school clinic. In this demonstration, all students are dummy data.

Sign in with Google

If you choose "Continue with Google", Google tells us only that you own the Google account and shares its email address. We use it only to confirm it is you when you log in. We do not read your Gmail, contacts or any other Google data.

How it is used and shared

Information is used only to run the clinic system. We do not sell it or share it with advertisers. It is stored in a Supabase database, and access is limited to logged-in staff according to their role.

Your choices

You can remove your linked Google account, change your password and change your profile picture under "My account". To delete your account, contact the school administrator.
