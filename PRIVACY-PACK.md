# EduHealthHub privacy pack (drafts for the school to review)

These are plain-language drafts for a school clinic system under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173). They are not legal advice.
Have the school head and, if possible, a lawyer or the school's DPO read them. Fill every [bracket]. `privacy.html` already
carries the matching public wording.

## 1. Data Protection Officer (DPO) designation
The school designates [Full name], [position], as Data Protection Officer for EduHealthHub, effective [date].
Contact: [email] / [phone]. The DPO: reads the Activity log at least [weekly / monthly]; answers access and correction requests; leads the
breach steps in section 5; keeps this pack up to date. Signed: [School head] ______ Date: ______

## 2. Parent / guardian consent (learners under 18)
**Learner:** [name] **Grade and section:** [ ]
[School name] keeps a health record for my child in EduHealthHub, a password-protected system used only by the school clinic staff
(doctor, nurse, dentist) and, for their own advisory section, the class adviser. It holds health history, screening and exam results,
dental chart, clinic visits and treatment, medicines given, referrals and uploaded health documents. It is used to care for my child at
school and for school health reporting. It is not sold or shared with advertisers. It is kept while my child is enrolled and
[retention period] afterwards. I may ask to see, correct or delete the record, or withdraw this consent, by writing to the DPO
([contact]). Records the school must keep by law or for my child's safety may still be kept.
I consent. Parent/guardian name: ______ Signature: ______ Date: ______
(Learners aged 18 and above sign for themselves.)

## 3. Staff confidentiality agreement (before any account is approved)
I will: use only my own account; keep my password and recovery codes private and never share them; look at a learner's record only for
clinic work; not copy, photograph, send or discuss records outside the school's approved channels; log out on shared computers; report
a lost phone or a suspected breach to the DPO the same day. I understand my actions are recorded in the Activity log and that misuse can
lead to school discipline and penalties under RA 10173. These duties continue after I leave.
Name: ______ Role: ______ Signature: ______ Date: ______

## 4. Retention schedule (the school decides; these are starting suggestions)
| Item | Keep | Then |
|---|---|---|
| Learner health record | while enrolled + [e.g. 5 years] | delete or archive securely |
| Uploaded health documents | same as the record | delete with it |
| Messages and attachments | [e.g. 1 school year] | delete |
| Activity log | [e.g. 2 years] | delete |
| Nightly backups | 7 days (automatic) | deleted automatically |
| Staff accounts | while employed | deactivate on the last day, delete after [e.g. 1 year] |
Who does it: [DPO or a named Doctor], reviewed each [June / year end].

## 5. If personal data is lost or exposed (breach steps)
1. Tell the DPO at once. Write down what happened, when, and which learners or staff may be affected.
2. Contain it: a Doctor opens **Accounts** and deactivates or resets the affected accounts and **Reset 2-step**; change the database keys if needed.
3. Check the **Activity log** to see what was opened or changed.
4. Within **72 hours**, notify the National Privacy Commission and the people affected (for learners under 18, their parents or guardians), saying what happened, what data, and what you are doing about it.
5. Record the incident and what was changed so it does not happen again.

## 6. Requests to see, correct or delete a record
The person writes to the DPO. The DPO confirms who they are (a parent or guardian for a minor), has a Doctor open the record,
and answers within [e.g. 15 working days]. Corrections are made in the learner's record; deletions follow section 4.
Log each request: date, who asked, what was done.
